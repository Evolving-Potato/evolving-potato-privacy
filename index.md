Privacy Policy — Football Brainiac

Policy draft dated 14 October 2026.  
Developer: **Evolving Potato**  
Contact: **[evopotatostudio@gmail.com](mailto:evopotatostudio@gmail.com)**

Football Brainiac stores game progress on your device and uses Google services for advertising and advertising privacy choices. This policy explains those different uses of information.

### Game information stored on your device

The game saves your Coins, Energy, Knowledge Shards, unlocked arenas and upgrades, quiz scores, question mastery, selected answers and mistake history, daily tasks and challenges, and related gameplay records. It also retains local transaction and reward records to resume play and avoid duplicate charges or rewards.

These records are used to operate the game and restore your progress between sessions. The current game does not upload its saved progress to an Evolving Potato server or offer account-based cloud saves. It does not ask you to register a Football Brainiac account or enter a name, email address or date of birth to play.

Progress remains in the app's local storage until it is replaced during play or the app's stored data is removed. Some recent history is bounded; other progress and reward records have no fixed automatic expiry. Saves are ordinary local files protected by your device's storage controls, rather than separately encrypted by the game.

### Google advertising and privacy services

The Android app integrates Google AdMob/Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform, using the Poing Studios bridge. Google services can process IP/general location, device or account-related identifiers, app/ad interactions and technical diagnostics, subject to the applicable configuration and privacy controls. They also manage consent choices and receive app/device information for privacy-message requests. This can include SDK-related crash information. [Google's advertising data guidance](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) explain provider practices.

Viewing a rewarded video is optional. However, advertising privacy checks and ad preparation can begin when the game opens, before you decide to watch a video. The game's own code does not include your saved balances, quiz answers or a player profile in ad requests. Google services are separate from the local save system.

The present development configuration uses Google's rewarded test advertisements. This does not mean that Google SDK communication is absent. The game waits for its privacy checks to resolve before making rewarded advertisements ready; if privacy handling fails, rewarded features remain unavailable.

Google processes information for its advertising, measurement, service and security purposes as described in its provider policies and the choices/settings that apply. Provider information may be processed outside your country. See [how Google uses information from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Google's retention and deletion practices are separate from the game's local storage and our email policy.

### Your advertising choices

Google's privacy service may present choices based on the applicable configuration and region. When it reports that privacy options are required, Football Brainiac provides **AD PRIVACY OPTIONS** on the Tasks screen. You can also use applicable Android advertising-ID controls and Google's advertising settings. Changing ad personalization does not necessarily stop all technical, consent-management, measurement or security processing. [Google's explanation of advertising controls](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Support and privacy correspondence

If you email **evopotatostudio@gmail.com**, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send, such as a name, message, screenshots or diagnostic attachments. We use this correspondence to respond to support/privacy inquiries, handle requests and resolve related issues.

Our policy is to keep support and privacy correspondence in **Gmail**, without copying it to another customer-support platform. We retain it for **up to 24 months after the last relevant correspondence**, unless legal obligations or an unresolved dispute require longer retention. We delete messages when no longer needed and honor applicable privacy deletion requests. Gmail's handling of information is also subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Deletion and security

You can remove local game progress by clearing Football Brainiac's app storage through your Android device settings; uninstalling normally removes app-private data as well. This loses local progress, and we do not provide a developer cloud copy from which to restore it. Device transfer or independently retained copies may behave differently. These actions do not delete information already processed by Google or emails you sent to us.

For requests relating to information you supplied directly to Evolving Potato, contact our privacy email. For information held by Google, refer to Google's privacy and account controls; we cannot promise removal of all provider records by deleting a game save.

Google documents encrypted transmission for its advertising SDK, and the privacy service uses HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is absolutely secure. Local game files rely on your device's protections. Development/device diagnostic logs can contain technical messages and limited gameplay information; the current game has no developer-operated automatic log uploader.

### Intended audience

Football Brainiac is intended for players **aged 13 and older**, primarily teenagers and adults. It is not specifically directed at children under 13. The game does not collect a birth date or verify a player's age. Its advertising/privacy treatment follows the applicable app configuration and Google controls. Contact us if you have an age-related privacy concern.
